Explore Every Screen of JW Ministry

Actual screenshots from the app. Browse all 8 screens — from the live ministry timer and Bible students list to pioneer calendar goals, territory cards, and OLED dark mode.

1. Start Tab & Live Timer

Track your ministry time down to the second (08:04:08). Pause, save to report, or reset in one tap. Increment Bible studies during the session, attach specific Bible students (e.g., Alex), write session notes (territory number, companion), and see total time spent today.

2. Bible Students Manager

Keep an organized list of your Bible students. Record the exact study publication, lesson, and section (for example: 'Enjoy Life Forever!, lesson 24, section 3') and add new students in seconds.

3. Service Year & Monthly Reports

Switch between service years (2025–2026, 2026–2027), monitor total service year hours and remaining hours, review every month with detailed session logs, study counts, student tags, and share your monthly report on the 1st.

4. Pioneer Calendar & Goal Progress

See your current role (Regular pioneer – 50 h, Auxiliary 15/30 h, or Publisher) and personal monthly target (e.g., 55 h). Visual progress bar shows hours completed this month, remaining monthly hours, and full service year balance alongside your daily schedule.

5. Territory Cards & Door-to-Door Log

Attach photos of territory maps or buildings (e.g., Territory A-8), filter addresses, and log every visit with date, visit status ('First visit', 'Not at home', 'Refusal'), and doorway notes like 'No intercom'.

6. Flexible Settings & Cloud Backup

Export or import full JSON backups via the top cloud buttons. Configure your service role, custom monthly hour target, September–August service year, 1st-of-the-month report reminders at 20:00, and return visit notifications after N days.

7. True Black OLED Dark Mode (Timer)

Designed for comfort during evening witnessing and battery saving on OLED iPhones. High-contrast purple accents keep controls clear in any lighting.

8. OLED Dark Mode (Settings & Goals)

Every screen, sheet, and widget seamlessly adapts to iOS system Light and Dark appearances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my ministry records and territory notes stored?

Exclusively on your device (iPhone or iPad). The app has no external database or cloud server, ensuring complete confidentiality of your personal notes and addresses.

How do I transfer my data to a new iPhone or iPad?

Open the Settings tab and tap the Cloud Upload icon in the top-right corner to export a full backup file (including territory photos) to iCloud Drive or AirDrop. On your new device, tap the Cloud Download icon to restore everything in one tap.

How do automatic return visit reminders work?

In Settings → Notifications, enable 'Remind about return visits to addresses' and set the interval (for example, 3 days). If you haven't logged a follow-up visit to an address within that period, the app will gently remind you.

Does the app support the September–August Service Year?

Yes! Both the Report tab and the Calendar summary calculate monthly and annual hours across the official September–August service year, including remaining hours toward your annual pioneer goal.
