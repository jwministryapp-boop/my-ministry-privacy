“JW Ministry” Gizlilik Politikası

This Privacy Policy explains how the “JW Ministry” mobile application (“the App”) handles user information. Our core principle is 100% privacy: all records you create are stored exclusively on your device.

1. Data Created and Stored in the App

All data in the App is entered voluntarily by the user for personal field service organization and planning:

  • Service time entries & reports: dates, duration, Bible study counts, and session notes.
  • Calendar schedules: planned ministry events, dates, times, and recurrence settings.
  • Bible students list: student names/labels and study publication progress notes.
  • Territories & addresses: territory names, house/apartment lists, visit history, notes, and territory card photos selected by the user from the Photo Library.
  • App settings: selected service role, personal monthly hour target, and notification preferences.

2. 100% Local On-Device Storage & No Third-Party Sharing

  • All data is stored solely on your iPhone or iPad within the secure iOS / iPadOS storage container.
  • The App requires no registration and no user account.
  • The developer has no access to your records, collects no analytics or location data, and never transmits or shares any data with external servers or third parties.

3. Device Permissions (Photos & Notifications)

  • Photo Library: requested only when you choose to attach a map or building photo to a territory card. Selected images are stored locally inside the App folder on your device.
  • Notifications: used exclusively for local on-device reminders (such as the 1st-of-the-month report reminder or return visit reminders).

4. Backups & Data Deletion

You may export a complete backup file (including territory photos) at any time via the standard iOS share sheet (for example, saving to your personal iCloud Drive or Files app). Uninstalling the App from your device permanently deletes all local App data.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the App, please contact the developer at: jwministry.app@outlook.com
