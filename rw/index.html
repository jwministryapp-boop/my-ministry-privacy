Shakisha buri Mugaragaza wa JW Ministry

Amashusho nyayo avuye muri porogaramu. Reba kuri ecran 8 zose - uhereye kumurimo wa minisiteri nzima hamwe nabanyeshuri ba Bibiliya kurutonde rwintego za kalendari, amakarita yubutaka, nuburyo bwa OLED bwijimye.

Imvugo yimbere kuri ecran: Ikinyarwanda

1. Tangira Tab & Live Timer

Kurikirana igihe cyawe cyo gukora kugeza kumunsi wa kabiri (08:04:08). Kuruhuka, kubika kubika raporo, cyangwa gusubiramo kanda imwe. Ongera wige Bibiliya mugihe cyamasomo, shyiramo abanyeshuri ba Bibiliya (urugero, Alex), andika inyandiko zamasomo (nimero yintara, umufasha), hanyuma urebe igihe cyose cyakoreshejwe uyumunsi.

Ingeso zo mu mwuka & Impeta ziterambere

Kurikirana ingeso za buri munsi, buri cyumweru, na buri kwezi (Umwandiko wa buri munsi, gusoma Bibiliya, gutegura inama, Gusenga Imiryango), fungura inyandiko ya buri munsi mu JW Library, hanyuma wongere ingeso gakondo.

Gahunda yo Gusoma Bibiliya

Urusobekerane rwibitabo 66 byose bya Bibiliya hamwe ninzira 5 zo gusoma (Classic, Chronologie, Amavanjiri Yambere, Iringaniza, Icya mbere), itariki yo kurangiriraho, hamwe no gusoma kanda 1 mubitabo bya JW.

2. Umuyobozi w'abanyeshuri ba Bibiliya

Komeza urutonde rwabanyeshuri bawe ba Bibiliya. Andika neza ibyatangajwe, isomo, nigice (urugero: 'Ishimire Ubuzima Iteka!, Isomo rya 24, igice cya 3') hanyuma wongere abanyeshuri bashya mumasegonda.

3. Umwaka wa serivisi & Raporo ya buri kwezi

Hindura hagati yumwaka wa serivisi (2025–2026, 2026–2027), ukurikirane amasaha yumurimo wose hamwe namasaha asigaye, suzuma buri kwezi ukoresheje ibisobanuro birambuye byamasomo, ibarwa yo kwiga, ibirango byabanyeshuri, hanyuma usangire raporo yawe ya buri kwezi kumunsi wa 1.

4. Kalendari y'abapayiniya & Intego y'Iterambere

Reba uruhare rwawe muri iki gihe (Umupayiniya usanzwe - 50 h, Umufasha 15/30 h, cyangwa Publisher) n'intego yawe ya buri kwezi (urugero, 55 h). Amashusho agaragara yerekana amasaha yarangiye muri uku kwezi, amasaha asigaye ya buri kwezi, hamwe numwaka wuzuye wa serivisi hamwe na gahunda yawe ya buri munsi.

5. Ikarita yintara & Urugi-Kuri-Urugi

Ongeraho amafoto yikarita yubutaka cyangwa inyubako (urugero, Teritwari A-8), aderesi ya aderesi, hanyuma wandike buri sura ufite itariki, gusura imiterere ('Gusura bwa mbere', 'Ntabwo ari murugo', 'Kwanga'), hamwe ninoti zumuryango nka 'Nta intercom'.

6. Igenamiterere ryoroshye & Igicu kibitse

Kwohereza cyangwa gutumiza muri JSON yuzuye ibicishije hejuru yibicu byo hejuru. Shyiramo uruhare rwa serivise, intego yisaha ya buri kwezi, Nzeri-Kanama umwaka wa serivisi, 1-ukwezi-kwibutsa raporo yibutsa 20h00, hanyuma usubire kumenyesha gusura nyuma yiminsi N.

7. Umukara nyawe OLED Yijimye (Timer)

Yagenewe guhumurizwa mugihe cyo guhamya nimugoroba no kubika bateri kuri iPhone ya OLED. Ibinyuranyo-bitandukanye cyane byijimye byijimye bikomeza kugenzura neza mumuri ayo ari yo yose.

8. OLED Yijimye (Igenamiterere & Intego)

Yubatswe kubatangabuhamya ba Yehova Umurimo wo mu murima & Umupayiniya

Uburyo Minisiteri JW ifasha abamamaji, abapayiniya bufasha, abapayiniya basanzwe, nabapayiniya badasanzwe gutegura umwaka wabo.

Minisiteri isanzwe kandi idasanzwe

Abapayiniya Bisanzwe & Bidasanzwe (Amasaha 50, 90 & 100 / Umwaka wa serivisi-Amasaha 600)

Gukora nk'umupayiniya usanzwe bisaba gukurikirana intego zawe zamasaha 50 buri kwezi hamwe nintego yawe yamasaha 600 Nzeri-Kanama. JW Ministry ihita ibara amasaha yawe yuzuye ya minisiteri, asigaye asigaye buri kwezi, hamwe niterambere ryumwaka wa serivisi. Shiraho intego yihariye ya buri kwezi (urugero, amasaha 55), teganya iminsi yumurimo muri Kalendari, kandi ugereranye amasaha yawe yateganijwe nigihe cyawe cyatanzwe.

  • Automatic 50 h buri kwezi & 600 h Nzeri - Kanama umwaka wa serivisi ukurikirana
  • Guhitamo isaha ya buri kwezi intego irenga (urugero, 55 h / ukwezi)
  • Amajyambere abiri yo kugereranya ugereranije namasaha yatanzwe
Abafasha b'abapayiniya n'abamamaza

Abapayiniya Bafasha (Amasaha 15 & 30) hamwe nabatangaza itorero

Kwinjira mubukangurambaga budasanzwe, gusura umugenzuzi wumuzunguruko, cyangwa igihe cyUrwibutso nkumupayiniya wungirije? Hindura uruhare rwawe kuri Pioneer Auxiliary (15 h cyangwa 30 h) mumasegonda. Abamamaji b'itorero barashobora kwandikisha amasomo ya buri munsi, bagakurikirana amasomo ya Bibiliya, kandi bagashobora kumenyesha mu buryo bwikora ku ya 1 ya buri kwezi saa 20h00 bityo ntuzibagirwe gutanga raporo yumurimo wawe.

  • Kanda kanda imwe kuri 15 h cyangwa 30 h Intego zubufasha bwa Pioneer
  • Guhita ukora raporo ya buri kwezi no kugabana ku ya 1
  • Kumenyesha kwibutsa witonze ku ya 1 ya buri kwezi saa 20h00
Inzu-Kuri-Inzu & Ifasi Yumuntu

Intara ku nzu n'inzu, Imashini itanga amazu & Gusura

Simbuza impapuro impapuro hamwe namakarita yububiko bwa sisitemu. Andika 'Gusura Bwa mbere', 'Ntabwo ari murugo', cyangwa 'Kwanga', shakisha mu turere twose ukoresheje aderesi y'umuhanda cyangwa izina ry'umuturage, kandi wakire ibyibutsa byikora mugihe kigeze cyo gusubirayo.

  • Amashanyarazi menshi yamagorofa kubwinjiriro no hasi

Ibibazo Bikunze Kubazwa

Nigute JW Ministry yuzuza JW Library kubahamya ba Yehova?

Mugihe JW Library rikoreshwa mugusoma Bibiliya, ibitabo, ninama zitorero, JW Ministry yubatswe mugukurikirana umurimo wawe wihariye: gutondekanya amasaha ya raporo yumurimo wo mu murima, gutegura gahunda yawe yubupayiniya isanzwe cyangwa igufasha (amasaha 15, 30, 50, cyangwa 100), gucunga inyandiko z’ubutaka bwite, no gukurikirana abanyeshuri ba Bibiliya.

Ni hehe inyandiko zanjye za minisiteri hamwe n'inoti z'ubutaka zibitswe?

Byumwihariko kubikoresho byawe (iPhone cyangwa iPad). Porogaramu nta base base yo hanze cyangwa seriveri yibicu, byemeza ibanga ryuzuye ry'inyandiko zawe na aderesi.

Nigute nshobora kohereza amakuru yanjye kuri iPhone cyangwa iPad nshya?

Fungura igenamiterere hanyuma ukande ahanditse Cloud Upload mugice cyo hejuru-iburyo kugirango wohereze dosiye yuzuye (harimo amafoto yubutaka) kuri iCloud Drive cyangwa AirDrop. Ku gikoresho cyawe gishya, kanda igishushanyo cya Cloud kugirango ugarure ibintu byose muri kanda imwe.

Nigute gusubiramo gusubira kwibutsa gukora?

Muri Igenamiterere → Kumenyesha, gushoboza 'Kwibutsa ibyerekeye gusubira kuri aderesi' hanyuma ushireho intera (urugero, iminsi 3). Niba utarigeze winjira gusura kuri aderesi muri kiriya gihe, porogaramu irakwibutsa witonze.

Porogaramu ishyigikira umwaka wa serivisi ya Nzeri - Kanama?

Yego! Byombi Raporo ya Raporo hamwe na Kalendari incamake ibara amasaha ya buri mwaka na buri mwaka mu mwaka wa serivisi wemewe wa Nzeri - Kanama, harimo amasaha asigaye ugana ku ntego yawe ya buri mwaka.

JW Ministry isimburwa neza kuri ServicePlanner cyangwa MyTime kuri iPhone?

Yego! JW Ministry irabungabunzwe cyane kubijyanye na iOS na iPadOS iheruka gusohora, hagaragaramo ibikorwa bya Live kuri Lock Screen na Dynamic Island, widgets yo murugo ya interineti, imashini itanga inyubako, Igenamigambi na Kalendari nyayo ikurikirana, hamwe na 1-kanda iCloud Drive.

Porogaramu ikora kumurongo rwose mugihe cyo gukora inzu ku nzu?

Rwose. Ibihe byose, ikarita yubutaka, inyandiko zamazu, hamwe ninyandiko zabanyeshuri ba Bibiliya zikora 100% kumurongo udakeneye amakuru ya mobile cyangwa Wi-Fi.

Nshobora guhindura ibikorwa byateganijwe muri minisiteri muri Kalendari mubyinjira muri Raporo?

Yego. Ihanagura gusa kuri gahunda iteganijwe ya minisiteri muri Kalendari hanyuma ukande 'Kumenyesha' - igihe kirahita cyinjira muri raporo yawe ya buri kwezi kandi kigakurwa mu masaha yawe asigaye.

JW Ministry ikubiyemo Gahunda yo Gusoma Bibiliya ihujwe na JW Library?

Yego! Munsi ya tab ya Habits kuri ecran yo gutangira, urabona igitabo cyibitabo 66-Gahunda yo Gusoma Bibiliya hamwe ninzira 5 zo gusoma (Classic, Chronologie, Amavanjiri Yambere, Kuringaniza Kuvanga, Bigufi Bwa mbere), itariki yo kurangiriraho, hamwe na kanda 1 'Soma muri JW Library' kumutwe utaha udasomwe hamwe na Text Daily.

Nigute abakurikirana ingeso zumwuka bakora?

Iragufasha kugenzura ingeso zo mu mwuka za buri munsi, buri cyumweru, na buri kwezi (Umwandiko wa buri munsi, gusoma Bibiliya, gutegura amateraniro, Kwiga ku giti cyawe, Gusenga Umuryango, JW Broadcasting, no gutanga raporo) hamwe nimpeta zerekana iterambere hamwe no gushyigikira ingeso gakondo.
